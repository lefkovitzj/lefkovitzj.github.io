ACM Meeting Records is a Python-based web application that allows for management of meetings for the student chapter of the Association of Computing Machinery at the University of Dayton, where I help lead the organization. This web app allows administrators to record meeting minutes, add attendees, create, update, and end meetings. Other organization members can mark attendance with a meeting passcode and view historical meeting information.

The system also allows the addition of meeting attachments for presentations or other relevant resources. The application implements multiple security features, including CSRF, reCAPTCHA, account validation, and multi-factor Authentication with both TOTP and recovery codes.
